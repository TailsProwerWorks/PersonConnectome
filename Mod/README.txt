<b><color=#FFD700>ABOUT</color></b>

Person Connectome attaches a bounded, real-time neural controller to a Human variation. It routes sensory input through the bundled MaleCNS-derived runtime and applies per-limb motor requests while respecting unsafe or terminal states.

<b><color=#FFD700>FEATURES</color></b>

• <color=#00FF00>Real-time sensory routing</color> for injury, oxygen, temperature, water, motion, contact, audio, projectiles, and environmental hazards
• <color=#00FF00>Per-limb motor control</color> with local capability and disconnection handling
• <color=#00FF00>Live telemetry</color> showing body state, sensory cues, neural activity, and applied requests
• <color=#00FF00>Bounded control</color> with stale-work dropping under load
