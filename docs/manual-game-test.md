# Manual People Playground test checklist

Source-linked tests use game doubles. The following native-game checks remain required; do not mark them passed based on an offline build.

1. Run `pwsh -NoProfile -File scripts/Test-GameCompilation.ps1` against the installed compiler's recorded references, then deploy with `scripts/Deploy-Mod.ps1`. Confirm exactly manifest scripts, `mod.json` and the PNG; enable the mod with shady-code rejection enabled and confirm compilation/loading succeeds. The Canvas/TextMeshPro overlay must compile without the previously reported GUIStyle/GUIContent errors. Offline compilation does not replace this native load check.
2. Spawn **Person Connectome (Active)**. Confirm a stock Human remains unmodified. The Brain page and documentation must identify the pinned 176422 neurons / 6287749 retained connections; missing/corrupt assets must disable control without a fallback.
3. Check the opaque top-center screen panel at 800×600, 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 3840×2160, plus window resizing and text-size extremes. Confirm the game's top-right buttons remain accessible at the default placement. Drag the title toward all four screen edges, shrink/grow with the lower-right handle, resize the game window, and collapse/re-expand; the header and handle must remain reachable. A- / A+ must render without missing glyphs. Scroll every page to its last line using the wheel and the visible scrollbar, including dragging the handle; no values should clip or sit beneath the scrollbar. The scrollbar should hide when content fits. Test darkness, walls, water, fire, mirroring, camera zoom/angle, ragdolling and head destruction. Use Prev/Next with several people and check the ID/coordinates, collapse/expand and pointer/scroll interaction. Initialize a disabled controller before an enabled controller and confirm the enabled controller still registers and renders the selected telemetry panel; disabling/removing a selected controller must select another available person and clean up its texture. UI clicks/scrolling must not unexpectedly manipulate world objects.
4. Inspect exact stock prefab limb roles/names and joint availability. Verify front/back channels differ under the documented convention, explicit left/right naming takes priority, unknown sides use their average, and jointless limbs are reported honestly.
5. Verify walking and per-limb motor requests. `REQUEST` is desired output; `LIMBS` reports driveable/applied counts and the applied walk request, not achieved physical motion. Missing grip components must not interrupt other limbs. While alive, jointless, broken, dismembered, disintegrated, paralysed or disconnected limbs should appear under `EXCLUDED`; a global native `IsCapable=false` state caused by water or another nonterminal condition must not disable healthy limbs. After death/brain death, the display should show one terminal `CONTROL: stopped` reason instead of repeating global capability shutdown for every limb. Confirm the native `Walk`, `Cower`, `Sit`, `Flat pose`, and `Stumble` context actions are absent for the controlled person while unrelated context actions remain. Disable/remove the controller and confirm those native pose actions return. Verify MN9 feeding activity does not request human pickup; brain grip requests should remain zero. Compare normalized walking intent with submitted walk-request and native motion on flat ground, without hazards. Check the default walking gain 2 and joint limit 30 deg/s for oscillation, stumbling or ineffective motion; these require tuning against the actual game, not just doubles.
6. Destroy/add limbs (including inactive children), sever limbs, and check no repeated exceptions or stale readings occur. Own detached limbs must not become nearby objects or external audio sources.
7. Test external physical-object audio, then mute/stop it. The current Sound signal must clear while the last-heard description remains with increasing real-time age; pause/unpause and change sources to verify the distinction. Walking alone must not fabricate `OBJECT AUDIO`; when audio is detected, `AUDIO` must identify the winning external physical/audio source and its distance/signal. Audio sources on the controlled person's root or limbs must be ignored. Repeat with orphan sources named Root, Root (3) and Root(Clone), including the reported 2.19-unit distance: ambiguous generic-root audio must be filtered, while a radio and identifiable other-person audio remain eligible. The name filter is conservative, not proof that a source belongs to this person. `ENCODER REQUESTS` shows actual modality amplitudes and queued input-neuron counts, separately from health/chemistry telemetry. Foot-floor impacts may correctly report `CONTACT IMPACT` and `vibration`; this is mechanical contact, not hearing. Check bounded collider surface proximity, `VISION` only for a line-of-sight target under ambient light, native projectile components, proprioception during motion/ragdolling, mechanical vibration distinct from hearing, `FALLING` during a downward airborne drop but not during horizontal walking or floor contact, and ambiguous zero-distance direction.
8. Test low oxygen outside water (`LOW OXYGEN`) and low oxygen underwater (`SUBMERGED HYPOXIA`), plus wetness without submersion. An immersed hand plus unrelated oxygen deficit is still only a submerged-hypoxia proxy, not verified drowning. Test fire/heat/cold, charge/shock, bleeding/wounds/blood loss, breakage/paralysis, brain injury and zombie state. Compare raw adrenaline against the native field and its normalized reading; do not expect or fabricate an injury-induced surge. Verify pain/shock do not cause mod-side adrenaline calming. With native adrenaline above 1, an active liquid-driven adjustment must not snap it down to 1; compare against native 0..20 bounds and normal decay.
9. Inject normal blood, Gorse blood, knockout poison, acid, adrenaline and healing liquids separately and as mixtures. Normal blood must not be a hazard, Gorse blood must register hazard exposure and knockout poison must register sedative exposure; these categories do not measure native effect strength. Removed liquids must not leave stale channels.
10. Verify `BRAIN INJURED` is not death; consciousness <=0.8 suppresses motor/grip/chemistry commands while neural sampling continues. Brain death and health <=0.001 must clear neural state, walking and joint requests, release grips and restore owned regeneration boosts. Genuine recovery starts from cleared state.
11. Check regeneration with a preexisting native/other-mod rate: smaller requests must not lower it. Stop/disable/remove the controller and verify its own boost is restored, while a newer external write remains untouched.
12. Check the source-linked invalid-health case corresponds to suspended control (`INVALID DATA`, `hp=unknown`) if reproduced with a diagnostic mod. Do not interpret invalid readings as death.
13. Under load, confirm fresh sensory inputs are serviced while processed work remains capped, `dropped`/`scheduler=OVERLOADED` report discarded stale overload work, and `fired` counts spikes from the latest neural tick. Check main-thread load time and steady frame time with one and several people; the cap is not a guaranteed frame-time bound.

14. Check the Brain page against captured tick numbers: white points must reflect actual sampled spikes; no positions should appear for missing soma data. Verify the 8,192-point sample, 141,781 located count, spike history and population fired/member bars. Pausing the game should increase input age without inventing new neural ticks; hidden/collapsed brain pages should stop texture updates. No panel value is a claim of biological Hz.
15. Observe requested motor traces under repeated stimuli and water entry/exit. Reversals should be bounded and terminal/invalid consciousness must clear immediately. There is no independent paddling oscillator or sensor-only escape request. Effective native movement, swimming and balance are not established by these offline changes. Native pose/standing mechanics still contribute; confirm and record this limitation rather than attributing their effects to the connectome.
16. Check the measured loop time and skipped game time with a slow frame and changed physics/tick rates. At 50 Hz physics, 20 Hz control should preserve its fractional remainder without catch-up bursts. Refractory duration is in neural ticks, not a biological-time guarantee.

Record native-game results separately from offline regression results. This is a fly graph mapped heuristically to a game human, not complete biological realism.

17. On fresh controlled Humans, exercise the 41 stock liquid IDs listed in api-compatibility.md (including obtainable pink-syringe variants), one at a time and in mixtures. Record IDs that cannot be produced through ordinary gameplay separately; do not mark them exercised. Compare the Senses concentration list with the native circulation inspector at the same time. Test knockout, adrenaline, acid, death, freeze, reanimation, life, painkiller and water-breathing syringes in particular. Water Breathing Serum must not show internal-water exposure; strength/durability/enhancing serums must not produce a stimulant-exposure request. Observe native oxygen/pain/health/zombie state separately, including effects persisting after the agent disappears. Test separated limbs and removal of liquids: fractions are per-limb maxima and names must clear after the next sample. Unknown/custom liquids must show exposure without guessed effects. Do not equate a concentration, a neural response or a motor request with effective resistance, treatment, immunity or poison recognition.

18. Move an identifiable audio source left/right/above the person and verify world-X bearing, including a source offset from its parent collider. It is not head-relative: rotating/mirroring the person does not rotate this convention. Stop/mute the source and verify the neural audio amplitude clears while history remains. Test visible approach/recession, equal target/person velocity, stationary targets, occlusion and missing rigidbodies; proximity alone must not drive LC4/LPLC2. Rotate/move a connected limb and compare normalized hinge readings against its native angle/speed; a disconnected or invalid joint must not supply load through another valid limb. Compare Brain encoder amplitudes for isolated touch, warm/cool, tilt and joint stimuli. Generic blood/poison/zombie values alone must not light up fabricated taste/odor receptors; an observed connected-limb health drop has its separately labeled engineered injury pathway. Record movement and frame times separately from neural spikes; full-graph offline stimulation already demonstrates overload remains possible.

19. Change global ambient brightness after a stable valid sample. Brightening should request the global ON channel; dimming should request OFF. Holding brightness constant should not continue requesting ON/OFF transients. First spawn/recovery must not fabricate a brightness transition. This uses global ambient light, not individual lamps, shadows or a spatial retinal image.

20. With one alive controlled Human, compare consecutive native connected-limb Health/InitialHealth readings against `damage-event`. A decrease emits once; holding old damage must not replay the event. Check healing, InitialHealth-changing serums, suspension/recovery, brain death and invalid samples. A fatal limb transition can emit once while the person survives; loss/disconnection before sampling may be missed. Compare `injury-proxy` in Brain with the documented Hill-derived encoder; native `pain` and adrenaline must not be relabeled as brain activity. No deliberate mod-side damage injection is part of the implementation.
21. Check native head/arm/leg/core contact separately, including held limbs, floor contact, water-only exposure and unknown/custom limb names. Regional input should follow known contact flags; broad impact/vibration may reach several groups. Verify missing/disconnected limbs do not supply stale regional contact.
22. Play ordinary low-tone and high-tone external audio using the game's available audio objects. After Unity has spectrum history, compare coarse bands; startup empty buffers must show unavailable with broad playback still working. Move, mute, stop and switch the strongest source. Verify own/Root audio remains excluded and old spectrum energy clears. Stereo channel 0 and coarse bins are limitations; do not expect accurate frequency analysis at the 100 Hz boundary.
23. Move visible objects toward, away from and across the person, using different bounds sizes. Compare geometric size/expansion/sweep and LC4/LPLC2 versus LC11/LC18 encoder requests. Test occlusion, darkness, equal velocities and rotating/deforming bodies. The rigid-bounds estimate is approximate; native body angular velocity under light drives the separate VS proxy.
24. Observe filtered forward/backward/turn activity, selected mode, normalized requests and submitted movement together. Brief neural gaps should no longer immediately cancel an active walking mode; the latest permitted request should be refreshed between neural ticks despite native one-unit-per-second decay, while sustained silence must expire it. Backward activity should preempt forward, and neural halt/brake or unsafe native state should clear requests immediately. Test default gain 2 with the active 0.3 floor on flat ground, slopes and water, plus broken/missing legs. Record actual distance, stumbles, oscillation and frame times; offline mode tests do not prove a usable native gait or balance.
