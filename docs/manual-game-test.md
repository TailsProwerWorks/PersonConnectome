# Manual People Playground test checklist

Source-linked tests use game doubles. The following native-game checks remain required; do not mark them passed based on an offline build.

1. Deploy with `scripts/Deploy-Mod.ps1`. Confirm exactly manifest scripts, `mod.json` and the PNG; enable the mod with shady-code rejection enabled and confirm compilation/loading succeeds.
2. Spawn **Person Connectome (Active)**. Confirm a stock Human remains unmodified. The overhead telemetry must show the pinned 176422 neurons / 6287749 retained connections; missing/corrupt assets must disable control without a fallback.
3. Check the overhead label on both screen sides and mirrored people, while standing, rotated and ragdolled. Destroy the head and confirm root fallback remains readable. The label must have no collision/control effect.
4. Inspect exact stock prefab limb roles/names and joint availability. Verify front/back channels differ under the documented convention, explicit left/right naming takes priority, unknown sides use their average, and jointless limbs are reported honestly.
5. Verify walking and per-limb motor requests. `REQUEST` is desired output; `LIMBS` reports driveable/applied counts and the applied walk request, not achieved physical motion. Missing grip components must not interrupt other limbs. While alive, jointless, broken, dismembered, disintegrated, paralysed or disconnected limbs should appear under `EXCLUDED`; a global native `IsCapable=false` state caused by water or another nonterminal condition must not disable healthy limbs. After death/brain death, the display should show one terminal `CONTROL: stopped` reason instead of repeating global capability shutdown for every limb. Confirm the native `Walk`, `Cower`, `Sit`, `Flat pose`, and `Stumble` context actions are absent for the controlled person while unrelated context actions remain. Disable/remove the controller and confirm those native pose actions return. Test actual pickup/drop.
6. Destroy/add limbs (including inactive children), sever limbs, and check no repeated exceptions or stale readings occur. Own detached limbs must not become nearby objects or external audio sources.
7. Test external physical-object audio, then mute/stop it. Walking alone must not fabricate `OBJECT AUDIO`; when audio is detected, `AUDIO` must identify the winning external physical/audio source and its distance/signal. Audio sources on the controlled person's root or limbs must be ignored. `INPUT` breaks `in` into injury, hazard, motion and arousal contributions. Foot-floor impacts may correctly report `CONTACT IMPACT` and `vibration`; this is mechanical contact, not hearing. Check bounded collider surface proximity, `VISION` only for a line-of-sight target under ambient light, native projectile components, proprioception during motion/ragdolling, mechanical vibration distinct from hearing, `FALLING` during a downward airborne drop but not during horizontal walking or floor contact, and ambiguous zero-distance direction.
8. Test low oxygen outside water (`LOW OXYGEN`) and low oxygen underwater (`SUBMERGED HYPOXIA`), plus wetness without submersion. An immersed hand plus unrelated oxygen deficit is still only a submerged-hypoxia proxy, not verified drowning. Test fire/heat/cold, charge/shock, bleeding/wounds/blood loss, breakage/paralysis, brain injury and zombie state.
9. Inject normal blood, Gorse blood, knockout poison, acid, adrenaline and healing liquids separately and as mixtures. Normal blood must not be a hazard, Gorse blood must be corrosive and knockout poison must register sedation. Removed liquids must not leave stale channels.
10. Verify `BRAIN INJURED` is not death; consciousness <=0.8 suppresses motor/grip/chemistry commands while neural sampling continues. Brain death and health <=0.001 must clear neural state, walking and joint requests, release grips and restore owned regeneration boosts. Genuine recovery starts from cleared state.
11. Check regeneration with a preexisting native/other-mod rate: smaller requests must not lower it. Stop/disable/remove the controller and verify its own boost is restored, while a newer external write remains untouched.
12. Check the source-linked invalid-health case corresponds to suspended control (`INVALID DATA`, `hp=unknown`) if reproduced with a diagnostic mod. Do not interpret invalid readings as death.
13. Under load, confirm fresh sensory inputs are serviced while processed work remains capped, `dropped`/`scheduler=REALTIME` report discarded stale overload work, and `fired` counts spikes from the latest neural tick. Check main-thread load time and steady frame time with one and several people; the cap is not a guaranteed frame-time bound.

Record native-game results separately from offline regression results. This is a fly graph mapped heuristically to a game human, not complete biological realism.
